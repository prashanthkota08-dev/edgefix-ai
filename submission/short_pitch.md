# Short pitch

**See it. Ask it. Fix it — offline.** EdgeFix AI helps field technicians connect an equipment photo and a maintenance question to local technical guidance, then shows the cited pages behind its suggested checks. CLIP supplies a visual similarity signal; local retrieval and evidence-grounded reasoning shape the response, including safety and uncertainty. The workflow has been verified on an Intel CPU. Snapdragon X Series is the intended deployment target, and target validation remains a next step.
